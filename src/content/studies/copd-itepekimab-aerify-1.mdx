## About the study

AERIFY-1 was a Phase 3 study of itepekimab, an investigational antibody that blocks interleukin-33 (IL-33), compared with placebo in former smokers with moderate-to-severe COPD. The main goal was to see whether it reduced moderate or severe COPD flare-ups. Lung function and respiratory symptoms were also measured.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 40 to 85 years old
- Had moderate-to-severe COPD and were former smokers

## Status at Veritas

This study has been **completed**. It is no longer enrolling, and it is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
