## About the study

PERSIST ASTHMA-1 is a Phase 3 study evaluating felcorekibart (GSK5784283), an investigational biologic, compared with placebo when added to usual asthma treatment. The main goal is to see whether it reduces asthma attacks that need oral steroids or a hospital stay over one year. Lung function, asthma control and quality of life are also measured.

## Who can participate

You may be eligible if you:

- Are 12 to 80 years old
- Have had a doctor's diagnosis of asthma for at least 12 months
- Use a medium- or high-dose inhaled steroid plus at least one other daily controller medicine
- Have had at least one asthma attack in the past year and still have uncontrolled symptoms
- Do not currently smoke or vape

Other criteria apply. A research coordinator will review eligibility with you, and a study doctor confirms it at a screening visit.

## What to expect

- A screening visit with breathing tests and an eligibility review
- Study visits over about one year
- Lung function testing and symptom questionnaires
