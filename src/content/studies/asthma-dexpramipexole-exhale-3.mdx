## About the study

EXHALE-3 was a Phase 3 study of dexpramipexole, an investigational once-daily oral medicine, compared with placebo over 52 weeks in people with severe eosinophilic asthma that was not well controlled on medium- or high-dose inhaled steroids. It evaluated safety, tolerability and effect on asthma.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 12 years of age or older
- Had severe eosinophilic asthma not controlled on medium- or high-dose inhaled steroids

## Status at Veritas

This study was **ended early by the sponsor** and is no longer enrolling. It is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
