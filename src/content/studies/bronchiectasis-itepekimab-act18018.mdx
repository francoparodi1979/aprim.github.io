## About the study

This Phase 2 proof-of-concept study (ACT18018) evaluated two dosing regimens of itepekimab, an investigational anti-IL-33 antibody, compared with placebo in adults with non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis. The main goal was to see whether it reduced the rate of moderate or severe flare-ups over 24 to 52 weeks of treatment.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 18 to 85 years old
- Had non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis with at least 2 moderate or 1 severe flare-up in the prior year

## Status at Veritas

This study has been **completed**. It is no longer enrolling, and it is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
