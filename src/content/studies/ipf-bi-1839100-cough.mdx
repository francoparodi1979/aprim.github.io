## About the study

This Phase 2 study tested different doses of BI 1839100, an investigational oral medicine, against placebo for 12 weeks in people with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) or progressive pulmonary fibrosis (PPF) who had a troublesome chronic cough. Cough was measured over 24 hours with a portable device, along with questionnaires and breathing tests.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Had IPF (age 40 or older) or progressive pulmonary fibrosis (age 18 or older)
- Had a chronic cough for more than 8 weeks

## Status at Veritas

This study was **ended early by the sponsor** and is no longer enrolling. It is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
