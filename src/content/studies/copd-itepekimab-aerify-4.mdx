## About the study

AERIFY-4 was a Phase 3 extension study for adults with COPD who had completed one of the earlier itepekimab COPD studies. It evaluated the long-term safety and tolerability of itepekimab, an investigational antibody that blocks interleukin-33 (IL-33), given every two or four weeks, and continued to track flare-ups and lung function.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Completed the treatment period of an earlier itepekimab COPD study (EFC16750 or EFC16819)
- Were 40 to 85 years old

## Status at Veritas

This study has been **completed**. It is no longer enrolling, and it is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
