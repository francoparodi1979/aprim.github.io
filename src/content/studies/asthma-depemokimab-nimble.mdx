## About the study

NIMBLE was a Phase 3 study for people with severe eosinophilic asthma who were already benefiting from mepolizumab or benralizumab. It tested whether switching to depemokimab (GSK3511294), an investigational long-acting biologic, controlled asthma attacks as well as staying on the current biologic.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 12 years of age or older
- Had severe eosinophilic asthma and had been benefiting from mepolizumab or benralizumab

## Status at Veritas

This study has been **completed**. It is no longer enrolling, and it is listed here as part of Veritas Clinical Research's track record as a research site.
