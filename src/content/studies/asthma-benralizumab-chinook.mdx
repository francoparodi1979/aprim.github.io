## About the study

CHINOOK is a Phase 4 study of benralizumab, an approved biologic for severe eosinophilic asthma, compared with placebo. It looks at whether treatment changes the structure of the airways (airway remodeling) and lung function over a 48-week treatment period, using airway biopsies and detailed CT imaging.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 18 to 70 years old
- Had severe asthma with high eosinophil counts that was not controlled on high-dose inhaled steroid plus a long-acting bronchodilator

## Status at Veritas

This study is **active but no longer enrolling new participants**. People already enrolled continue their study visits.
