## About the study

This Phase 2a study is evaluating SAR445399, an investigational treatment, compared with placebo in adults with non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis. The main measure is the change in mucus plugging of the airways seen on a high-resolution chest CT scan after 24 weeks. Lung function and flare-ups of bronchiectasis are also tracked.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 18 to 80 years old
- Had bronchiectasis confirmed on chest CT, with daily mucus production
- Had at least 2 flare-ups treated with antibiotics in the prior year

## Status at Veritas

The sponsor has **temporarily paused** this study (listed as "Suspended" on ClinicalTrials.gov), and it is not enrolling new participants. If you are already taking part, our study team will contact you about next steps — you can also call your coordinator directly.
