## About the study

PERFORM is a real-world (pragmatic) Phase 4 study comparing a once-daily triple-therapy inhaler, fluticasone furoate/umeclidinium/vilanterol (FF/UMEC/VI), with the usual-care inhaled steroid and long-acting bronchodilator (ICS/LABA) inhalers in adults whose asthma is not well controlled. Participants are followed for 52 weeks, with the main analysis at 24 weeks.

## Who took part

The study enrolled people who:

- Were 18 to 75 years old
- Had asthma that was not well controlled, either untreated or on a daily ICS or ICS/LABA inhaler

## Status at Veritas

This study is **active but no longer enrolling new participants**. People already enrolled continue their study visits.
